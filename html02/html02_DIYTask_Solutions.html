Ingridients

before you start make sure you have these few things

  • butter 1 stick
  • sugar 1 cup
  • 2 large eggs
  • vanilla extract 1 tsp
  • flour 3/4 cup
  • salt 1/2 tsp
  • cocoa powder 1/2 cup

Making the brownies

  1. preheat oven to 350°F
  2. melt the butter and let it cool
  3. whisk the sugar and eggs for 1-2 minutes
  4. stir the vanillia extract and melted butter untill smooth
    • add choc chips here if you want to
  5. add the cocoa powder flour and salt
  6. fold untill flour dissapears with a spatuala
  7. preheat oven to 350°F
  8. line 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper and grease it
  9. spread the batter evenly
  10. bake for 20-25min
  11. test the doneness
  12. let the cool untill slicing them into clean slices
for darily free use coconut oil instead of butter. you can also use brown sugar. use gluten free baking flour.
makes 9-16 brownies
240 calories per serving
about 11g of fat
about 32g carbohydrates
about 23g of sugar
about 3g protien

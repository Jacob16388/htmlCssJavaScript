Recipie: Best Brownies

soft chewy brownies with choc chips

prep time:15 minutes
bake time:10 minutes
makes:8 brownies

These brownies are best enjoyed warm

ingridients

  • 2 cupsflour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa

instructions

  1. preheat oven to 350°F
  2. mix all ingridients
  3. put in oven
